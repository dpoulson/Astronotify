# Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

*Last updated: October 2026*

Astronotify is committed to absolute privacy, minimal data retention, and zero commercial data harvesting. We believe observing the night sky should never require sacrificing personal privacy.

---

## 1. Zero Tracking & No Data Harvesting
- **No Third-Party Trackers:** We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, Facebook/Meta pixels, or browser fingerprinting technologies.
- **No Behavioral Profiling:** We never monitor or log your activity across other sites or applications.

---

## 2. Purpose-Bound Data Usage
Your data is used strictly and exclusively to operate the core features of Astronotify:
- **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, and encrypted password (or Google OAuth ID) are used solely to authenticate your account and transmit your requested alert emails.
- **Observing Locations & Coordinates:** Latitude, longitude, elevation, Bortle rating, and custom threshold limits are used solely to query meteorological weather forecasts (Open-Meteo) and model orbital transit corridors for the International Space Station (SGP4 algorithm).
- **No GPS Telemetry:** We never track your device GPS in real-time in the background. Coordinates are saved only when you explicitly create or update an observing location.

---

## 3. Strict No-Marketing Policy
- **No Data Selling:** We will never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information or location data to any third party or data broker.
- **No Promotional Spam:** We never send marketing newsletters, affiliate advertisements, or sponsored pitches.
- **Only Essential Emails:** The only emails you receive from Astronotify are:
  1. Your active astronomical alerts (clear skies, ISS solar/lunar transits). Each email includes a direct, one-click unsubscribe link.
  2. Essential account security messages (email confirmation, password resets).
  3. Rare, critical service announcements regarding major platform updates or essential operational changes.

---

## 4. Full User Control & Right to Erasure
You have full ownership of your data and can permanently delete your account at any time:
- **How to Delete:** Navigate to **Profile Settings &rarr; Delete Account & Data**.
- **What is Purged Immediately:**
  - Account profile, email, authentication tokens, and credentials.
  - All saved observing sites and coordinates.
  - All cached weather conditions and predicted transit calculations.
  - All active browser sessions and password tokens.
- **Non-Personal Stability Metrics:** Anonymous, aggregated numerical system performance counters (e.g., total transit computations run) remain preserved without any user identification to ensure platform stability.

---

## 5. Third-Party Technical Processors
- **Open-Meteo:** Receives latitude and longitude coordinates in automated backend requests to provide numerical forecast models. No user names, emails, or personal identifiers are transmitted.
- **CelesTrak / Space-Track:** Provides public NORAD orbital element sets downloaded centrally.
